About

I was born in Australia and moved to Spain at the age of nine. Ever since I can remember, video games have been one of my biggest interests—not just playing them, but understanding how they work and how they're built.

I studied a dual degree in Computer Engineering and Business Administration at Universidad Complutense de Madrid. While the business side was never where my real interests lay, it did give me useful foundations in areas such as finance, statistics, and quantitative analysis. The engineering side, however, confirmed what I had suspected for years: I enjoy building things, solving technical problems, and understanding complex systems.

Outside of work, much of my time goes into game development. I'm particularly interested in graphics, physics, simulation, and the technical challenges involved in creating interactive worlds. I enjoy exploring how mathematics, programming, and physical systems can be translated into software, whether that's through game mechanics, rendering techniques, or simulation systems.

More broadly, I'm fascinated by technology that combines engineering with creativity. The problems that interest me most are usually the ones that require both analytical thinking and experimentation, where there isn't a predefined solution and the process of figuring things out is part of the appeal.
